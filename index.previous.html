The Mind

Play every card from 1 to 100 in ascending order — without talking.

How to play
  • Each level n, everyone gets n cards.
  • Play your lowest card when you feel it's next. No turns, no talking.
  • If a card is played while someone holds a lower one, those lower cards are discarded and the team loses a life.
  • Throwing star: everyone discards their lowest card face up (all must agree).
  • Rewards: star after levels 2, 5, 8 — life after levels 3, 6, 9 (max 5 lives, 3 stars).
  • Controls: click your lowest card or press Space. Press S to propose a star.
